what is bootstrap?

Bootstrap provides an easy-to-use framework of ready-made styles, layout tools, and interactive components, allowing developers to create websites and applications that are visually appealing, functionally rich, and accessible out of the box.

SASS ->  Syntactically Awesome Style Sheets (eg -> styles.scss)

(components & container & brake pointers & colors & grid) are the 2 most important topics in bootstrap


1)bootstrap   -> components (step -1)

2)bootstrap   -> containers | grid system (step - 2)

3)bootstrap   -> breakpoint | colors (step - 3)



website to learn bootstrap -> "https://getbootstrap.com/"
